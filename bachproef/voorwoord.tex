\chapter*{\IfLanguageName{dutch}{Woord vooraf}{Preface}}%
\label{ch:voorwoord}

When I first joined TrustBuilder in 2023 for a summer job, I was placed in the professional services team---working with the workflow engine to add custom functionality to the product. The system was capable, but I knew it could be so much more potent if it were given some extra love. I passionately talked about my ideas of what could be and I was wildly interested in how people experienced workflows in their current form.

A few months later, when I reached out again for an internship and research subject, I was delighted to hear they wanted me to look into their workflow system. As my internship assignment, I would be allowed to figure out the conceptual model of the next generation of workflows. My research subject would be figuring out what language to use for creating such extensions. I was given tremendous freedom and trust, and was really allowed to take ownership of the project. 

The amount of freedom was a double-edged sword though, because although I would be able to widely explore all possibilities, that also meant my assignment was not concretely scoped and defined. It was not always easy to figure out where I wanted to go with this, and there was no clear goal in mind in the beginning, but I am incredibly proud of what I have been able to realize.

I would like to thank my co-supervisor Yannick De Backer for the immense amount of trust he placed in me to work on such a fundamental project for the company. Naums Mogers and my brilliant seniors at TrustBuilder, Erkam Guresen and Peter Surnak, also granted me many insightful conversations which were always sure to lead to an epiphany. Last but not least, I am especially grateful to my supervisor Jan Willem for his continued support and guidance throughout my writing of this thesis. Completing it was no easy feat, and his input and feedback have been invaluable in getting it past the finish line.
